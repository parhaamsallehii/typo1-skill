# Reference vocabulary

All three supplied files are motion-only, not split-screen presenter examples.
The 50/50 geometry comes from the user's instruction. Keep the original aspect
ratio while inspecting. The three original files and hashes are in
`../assets/references/manifest.json`.

## Reference 1 — travel/assistant product film (1280×720, 15s)

Observe a near-white/cool pastel field; a tiny raised rounded input capsule;
multicolor glow that becomes a larger gradient field; a small glowing AI chip;
single-line black typography with an emphasized colored/selectable destination;
a fan of three raised image cards with yaw/depth; and a glass prompt/button finish.
The camera approaches UI details, then pulls back to explain a state. Short control
responses lead the motion; text stays readable at its landed state.

## Reference 2 — shopping assistant (720×720, 11s)

Observe sparse black type on white; a photographic item with pink selection
handles; a cursor-led selection; a thin pink-edged prompt plane in perspective;
analysis/status lines; and a depth stack of item/photo cards. Small vivid accents
have large blank surroundings. Avoid adding fake graphs or large dashboards.

## Reference 3 — visual discovery (1280×720, 22s)

Observe a compact icon dock and pointer, a brand-motivated zoom, a glass search
surface over a photographic meadow, close camera passes over text/controls, a
wall of colorful cards with parallax, word-by-word typographic emphasis and a
clean final mark. Product identity motivates color changes. Do not copy that
specific brand or unrelated card imagery into a Higgsfield explainer.

## Practical motion starting points

These are authoring estimates for new content, not recovered AE graph values:

- UI entrance: roughly .35–.65s with an expo/power4 tail-heavy ease-out.
- Camera handoff: roughly .25–.4s, accelerating into an object or following a
  pointer, then a decelerating landing. Maintain a clear visual connection.
- Pointer press: roughly .08–.13s down, .12–.2s release; a small response, not a
  large cartoon bounce.
- Card fan: stagger roughly .07–.14s; separate by depth and yaw, then settle.
- Perspective: keep the main reading surface near frontal at its reading moment.
  Use oblique angles for travel/transition. Do not keep all text severely foreshortened.

At lower-panel reference size 1080×960, use headlines around 48–72px and short UI
labels around 28–42px, then inspect at phone width. Use near-white #f5f5f7, white,
near-black #12131b, medium gray #6a6e7b and purposeful pink/violet/blue selection
accents. These colors are new-content starting values grounded in the supplied
reference appearance, not measurements of an original editable project.

For fast motion, inspect several consecutive frames. A frame still cannot establish
ease, velocity, overshoot or blur. Render one full transition, not only hero stills.
